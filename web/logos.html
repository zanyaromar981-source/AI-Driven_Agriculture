Farm Doctor — 50 logo concepts

Hand-drawn SVG marks for an AI farm advisor for the Kurdistan Region: satellite fields, 10-day weather, plant photos, one doctor who explains it in Sorani. Every mark is built from flat shapes, uses at most two palette colours, and still reads in a single colour.

A · Field + satellite

The field as seen from orbit: tiles, furrows, contours and the thing that watches them.

01

Orbit Field زەوی

A hex field tile with a satellite orbit passing behind it; the field is the planet.

02

Scan Beam چاودێری

Pictogram: a satellite sweeps a dashed cone over three widening crop rows.

03

Pixel Plot کێڵگە

A Sentinel-style pixel grid; gold pixels are the stressed patch, one cell is the satellite watching.

04

Furrow Eye چاوی کێڵگە

One colour. An eye whose lower lid is plowed furrows; the pupil is a hex map cell.

05

Contour Field زەوی

Topographic contour lines closing on a green heart of the field; a satellite dot overhead.

06

Hex Map نەخشە

Seven hex cells of the monitoring grid; the one that lights up gold is your field.

07

Dish & Sprout

A receiver dish doubles as a seed bed; the sprout grows at the focal point under the signal.

08

Tilted Plot زەوی

Abstract: a plot in perspective with converging rows, and a satellite glint in the corner.

09

Signal Rows

One colour. Contour-plowed rows seen from above are also a signal fanning out from one plant.

10

Field F

Monogram: an F cut out of a field tile, with a signal ping at the corner. App-icon ready.

Top 10 picks

  1. 09 Signal Rows — one colour, two meanings (plowed rows and a signal), zero cliché; scales to a favicon.
  2. 41 Jim Grain — a real Sorani letter with a wheat grain as its dot; owns the name "Jutyar" visually.
  3. 34 Ten-day Dial — the only mark that encodes the product's 10-day forecast literally, and it still reads as a sun.
  4. 11 Leaf Stethoscope — says "doctor for plants" in one line without a cross or a pill.
  5. 22 Wheat Stamp — negative-space wheat in a disc; feels like an official seal, good for the ministry side.
  6. 45 Sun Field — the 21-ray sun with furrows is instantly local and still calm and geometric.
  7. 04 Furrow Eye — satellite watching, hex pixel, furrows: the whole pipeline in one line drawing.
  8. 06 Hex Map — honest about the technology (grid monitoring) and app-icon friendly.
  9. 31 Rain Leaf — the strongest single-shape mark if the product is named Baran.
  10. 20 Advice Bubble — the voice of the AI doctor; pairs naturally with a chat-first mobile app.

All marks are hand-written inline SVG on a 100-unit grid, shown at 160×160. Letter marks 41–43 are drawn as strokes; 48 and 50 use the Noto Sans Arabic web font and should be converted to outlines before production.
