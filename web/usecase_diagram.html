Farm Doctor: use case diagram

Actors on the left, the system boundary in the middle, external AI and data on the right. Thick outlines = the 5 core AIs.

actor uses includes / extends external AI or data feeds itthick ellipse = one of the 5 core AIs

Inside the boundary

  • 5 core AIs: Field Eye, Weather Planner, Plant Doctor, Season check, Neighbour Watch
  • Add-ons: Dam Watch, Damage Proof, Soil water, Farmland loss and sown area, Animal Health, Market Watch, Label reader
  • The Doctor layer (Claude, Sorani)
  • Farmer app, Ministry web map, public page, case log

Outside, used not built

  • Satellites and weather models (ESA, NASA, ECMWF, Google)
  • Claude, Chirp, SAM, Prithvi, WorldCereal
  • KRSO and WFP statistics
  • Extension officers and vets: the system hands cases to them

Out of scope

  • Long-range forecasts (season, El Niño, climate)
  • Pesticide or fertilizer doses
  • Yield in tonnes, soil fertility maps, price forecasts
  • Credit, insurance, fraud flags, food-safety photo checks
  • Any hardware
