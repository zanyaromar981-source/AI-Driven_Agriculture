# Kurdistan Region agriculture: the big, well-known problems (2023–2026) and existing digital/AI work

Research date: 2026-10-07. KRI = Kurdistan Region of Iraq. 1 dunam = 2,500 m² (0.25 ha). IQD = Iraqi dinar.
Source quality note: items marked "(snippet)" were seen only in search-result text and could not be confirmed by opening the page (often a 403 error). Everything else was read on the source page.

## Q1. What are the top farming problems by public attention and economic size? (ranked, with numbers, events, who is responsible)

### Takeaway
The four problems that dominate KRI news and money are linked. (1) Drought and rain-fed wheat collapse (2025: about 2 million dunams failed, about 800,000 t of wheat lost). (2) Baghdad's wheat quota and payment fight (quota 700k → 400k → 290k/400k t; tens of billions of IQD paid late). (3) Low reservoirs plus Iranian upstream dams (Dukan at 24% and a 20-year low in June 2025, then overflowing in March 2026). (4) Groundwater over-pumping through about 28,000 illegal wells. A fifth, market and price collapse (smuggled imports, plus Baghdad blocking Kurdish produce in 2026), is close behind. Crop fires, pesticides, farmland loss to housing, pests, youth leaving farming and pump electricity are smaller, older, or not well evidenced for 2023–2026.

### Cited Findings

#### RANK 1: Drought and rain dependency of wheat/barley (largest by money and attention)
- June 2025: "Nearly two million dunams of rain-fed farmland" in KRI failed. About 3 million dunams are usually planted with winter crops, and about 2 million of those depend only on rain. Nearly half of rain-fed farmers chose not to plant, and those who did "lost most of their production". KRI usually produces >1 million t of wheat a year; 2025 was expected to fall below that. Spokesperson: Hiwa Ali (KRG Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources, MoAWR). — [Rudaw, 9 Jun 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/090620251)
- By province (2025 vs 2024): Erbil about 1 million t in 2024, expected to halve. Duhok 430,000 t → about 250,000 t. Rain was 215 mm vs 980 mm the year before. — [Rudaw search summary of the same coverage](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/090620251) (snippet). Conflicts with Peregraf's 2024 KRI average of 653 mm ([Peregraf, 14 Aug 2025](https://peregraf.com/en/investigation/9492)); the two likely measure different stations or areas.
- Iraq-wide wheat output fell by about 2.5 million t in 2025, **including about 800,000 t in KRI**. Nineveh alone was down about 1 million t (Haider Karrawi, DG of the Iraqi Grain Trading Company). — [Peregraf, 10 Jun 2025](https://peregraf.com/en/news/9102)
- Some losses were offset by about 300,000 t held in silos and by "a large number" of farmers switching to pivot (groundwater) irrigation. — [The New Region](https://thenewregion.com/posts/2434) (snippet)
- AFP case study: a farmer lost about $5,700 when winter wheat on 13 ha failed. — [AFP via Arab News, 21 Jun 2025](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2605253/middle-east)
- Earlier 2025 damage also came from cold weather combined with lack of rain. — [Rudaw, 4 Apr 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/040420251) (headline only)
- **Boom-bust:** 2026 was very wet. 22 of 25 KRI dams overflowed, and farmers expected yields of 15–20 times the seed. — [Rudaw, 25 Apr 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/250420261). About 3.3 million dunams of wheat were planted, with 1.5–2 million t expected. — Rudaw 2026 coverage (snippet; not confirmed on page)
- Baseline: KRSO records 4,409,376 dunams of wheat and 509,405 dunams of barley planted in 2022–23. — [KRSO agriculture indicators](https://krso.gov.krd/en/indicator/agriculture) (snippet; page returned 403). This conflicts with Rudaw's "about 3 million dunams of winter crops"; the definitions probably differ.
- Iraq-wide (labelled): in Nov 2025 Iraq halved its winter plan versus 2024, to 1 million dunams on surface water plus 3.5 million dunams on groundwater. This was the fifth straight year of water shortage. — [Capital FM / AFP, Nov 2025](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2025/11/iraq-limits-agricultural-plan-due-to-drought/)
- Responsibility: KRG MoAWR (planning, seeds, dams); the Iraqi federal Ministry of Agriculture sets the national "agricultural plan" that decides whose wheat Baghdad buys at full price (see Rank 2).

#### RANK 2: Baghdad wheat purchase quota cuts and late payments (biggest political and farmer-income fight)
- Baghdad's wheat purchases from KRI: **700,000 t (2024) → 400,000 t (2025) → 290,000 t first offered in 2026**. — [Rudaw, 25 Apr 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/250420261)
- 2025: Baghdad bought 400,000 t at 850,000 IQD/t, worth >300 billion IQD (about $229M). By province: Sulaimani + Halabja 158,000 t, Erbil 122,000 t, Duhok about 118,000 t. Payments came in instalments: 57 billion IQD first (Sulaimani/Halabja 25B, Erbil 20B, Duhok 12B), then 15.5 billion IQD as the second, reported on 14 Oct 2025. Erbil asked Baghdad to buy the whole harvest. KRG also handed out 3,254 t of subsidized seed wheat. — [Rudaw, 14 Oct 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/14102025)
- KRG Trade Minister: **83 billion IQD of 2025 farmer money was still held by Baghdad**. — [Channel8](https://channel8.com/english/news/65232) (snippet; date not confirmed)
- 2026: KRG Agriculture Minister **Begard Talabani** called Baghdad's policy "discriminatory". Baghdad first offered 292,000 t and then raised it to **400,000 t: in-plan wheat at 700,000 IQD/t and out-of-plan wheat at 500,000 IQD/t** (down from 850,000 in 2025). The PM's office authorized the finance ministry to settle outstanding farmer debts and froze land-lease and irrigation fees. — [Rudaw, 5 May 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/middleeast/iraq/050520264); [Rudaw business, 5 May 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/business/05052026). KRG gave out 11,000 t of subsidized seed for 2026 (same source).
- About 85% of Duhok's wheat (estimated at 400,000 t) may go unsold to the state in 2026. The Faida silo director said: "Procurement quotas allocated to the Kurdistan Region since 2017 have not reflected actual production levels." — [Shafaq News, 23 Jun 2026](https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/85-of-Duhok-wheat-crop-may-remain-unsold)
- KRG response: 4 silos with 160,000 t capacity built for 84 billion IQD, more planned (Duhok, Zakho, Kifri, Koya, Harir), and private-sector milling into flour for export. — [KRG DMI, Oct 2025](https://gov.krd/dmi-en/activities/news-and-press-releases/2025/october/krg-strengthens-food-security-with-expanded-wheat-storage-and-marketing-support/) (snippet; 403)
- Nearby (Kirkuk, outside KRG administration): farmers protested late wheat payments and Baghdad paid after a crackdown on the protests. — [Kurdistan24](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/opinion/894536/kirkuk-farmers-protest-delayed-wheat-payments-accuse-baghdad-of-discriminatory-policy); [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Iraq-settles-farmer-payments-following-protest-crackdown) (snippets)
- Responsibility: Iraqi federal Ministry of Trade (Grain Board), federal Ministry of Agriculture (national plan), federal Ministry of Finance (payments); KRG MoAWR and KRG Ministry of Trade negotiate.

#### RANK 3: Reservoirs (Dukan, Darbandikhan) and upstream dams in Iran
- **Dukan, June 2025: 1.6 billion m³ out of 7 billion (24%), the lowest in about 20 years.** Satellite imagery showed the lake surface shrank 56% between May 2019 and June 2025. Dukan got 220 mm of rain that winter against a typical 600 mm. The dam director (Kochar Jamal Tawfeeq) blamed climate change and upstream damming of the Little Zab in Iran. About 4 million people downstream (Sulaimani, Kirkuk) were affected, and Kirkuk treatment plants got 40% less water. — [AFP via Arab News, 21 Jun 2025](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2605253/middle-east); also [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3315334/iraqs-dukan-reservoir-drops-20-year-low-forcing-rationing) and [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/06/water-levels-plummet-drought-hit-iraqi-reservoir)
- Dukan hydropower stopped because the water was too low. — [Rudaw, May 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/030520252) (snippet)
- Raparin/Pishdar, April 2025: **Little Zab flow down 75% (130 → under 36 m³/s)**. The Sangasar irrigation project lost about 80% of its water, hitting 8,000 dunams; 7,000+ dunams of other farmland were affected. Raparin has 4.5 million dunams of farmland in total. Official: Sparda Mohammed, Raparin water resources directorate. — [Rudaw, 7 Apr 2025](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/070420252)
- Iranian dams: 16 dams on the Sirwan, the largest being Daryan (2018), 28 km from the border. The Kolsa dam (Sardasht) is linked to an 80% drop in the Little Zab. — [MEI](https://mei.edu/publication/water-scarcity-could-lead-next-major-conflict-between-iran-and-iraq/); [Kurdshop](https://kurdshop.net/en/geography/3499) (snippets). In 2020 Iran fully cut the Little Zab and largely cut the Sirwan, and officials warned of a "catastrophe" (2020, older). — [Rudaw, Aug 2020](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/190820201); [Rudaw, Aug 2020](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/230820201-amp)
- KRI dam storage: 21 dams (3 large), storing about 3.3 billion m³ against a need of about 12 billion m³ a year. Total incoming water fell from 35.7 billion m³ (2016) to 13.3 billion m³ (2022). — [Peregraf investigation, 14 Aug 2025](https://peregraf.com/en/investigation/9492)
- **2026 reversal:** Darbandikhan overflowed (22 Mar 2026), as did all small and medium dams and three Duhok dams. Dukan rose 17 m since January. Darbandikhan holds up to 2.6 billion m³. — [Rudaw, 22 Mar 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/220320261); [Peregraf](https://peregraf.com/en/news/11264). KRI stored more than 9 billion m³ ahead of summer 2026. — [Channel8](https://channel8.com/english/news/58984) (snippet). Officials expected no summer 2026 shortage. — [Rudaw, 18 Apr 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/middleeast/iraq/180420261) (headline)
- Responsibility: KRG MoAWR (dams directorate); Iraqi federal Ministry of Water Resources handles Iran/Turkey talks; Iran (upstream).

#### RANK 4: Groundwater over-pumping and illegal wells
- **28,410 illegal wells and 26,818 legal wells in KRI.** Illegal wells by area: Sulaimani 10,000+, Erbil 6,000, Garmiyan 1,500, Duhok 500. Over half of all wells are used for farming. Erbil's water table fell about 500 m (wells were about 120 m deep in 1996 and about 700 m now). Sulaimani fell about 20–30 m; Sitak about 200 m. — [Peregraf, 14 Aug 2025](https://peregraf.com/en/investigation/9492). Note: the same article also says Erbil and Sulaimani each have 20,000+ legal wells, which does not add up to 26,818, so treat the totals as approximate.
- Springs (2022, KRSO data quoted by Peregraf): 5,276 springs, of which 2,500 were active and **1,650+ had dried up**. 433 of 1,500+ qanats had dried up. — [Peregraf](https://peregraf.com/en/investigation/9492)
- Raparin: 890 licensed artesian wells vs **5,000+ unlicensed shallow wells**, with a further drop of about 10 m expected in 2025. — [Rudaw, 7 Apr 2025](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/070420252)
- Older figures: Erbil had 9,000 official and 5,000 illegal wells, and well depth went from about 120 m to 500 m (2021). — [Rudaw, 2021](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/04072021) (snippet). Mala Omar fell 100 m and Shaqlawa 60 m (2018). — [Rudaw, 2018](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/190220183-amp)
- Feb 2026: Iraq's Groundwater Directorate reported drops of 60–70 m in Iraq and KRI, and up to 100 m in places. — [Rudaw Turkish, 18 Feb 2026](https://rudaw.net/turkish/lifestyle/18022026) (snippet)
- Link to Rank 1: farmers move to pivot irrigation in dry years, which adds to groundwater draw (see The New Region, Rank 1).
- Responsibility: KRG MoAWR (well licensing), governorate water directorates.

#### RANK 5: Market and price collapse (imports and smuggling in; Baghdad blocking Kurdish produce out)
- The KRG runs seasonal import bans: apples (Aug 1–Oct 30), pomegranates (Aug 15–Jan 31), grapes (permanent), and lettuce, cauliflower, cabbage and others. — [KRG DMI, May 2023](https://gov.krd/dmi-en/activities/news-and-press-releases/2023/may/krg-implements-import-ban-on-agricultural-produce-to-protect-local-farmers/); [Iraq Business News](https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/?p=46228)
- Aug 2025: tomatoes, potatoes and cucumbers were being **smuggled in despite the bans**, mainly through Sulaimani border crossings into Garmiyan markets. Wholesale prices: tomatoes 250–600 IQD/kg, potatoes 250 IQD/kg. Erbil and Duhok produce 700,000 t of potatoes a year. — [Rudaw, 17 Aug 2025](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/170820251)
- Halabja tomatoes, July 2025: 100 IQD/kg, which equals the cost of the crate. One farmer got 25,000 IQD for over 2 t. Halabja's agriculture DG was quoted. — [Rudaw, 25 Jul 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/25072025). In 2024 Iraq halted tomato imports after Kurdish farmer protests, in which farmers destroyed crops. — [Rudaw, 9 Sep 2024](https://www.rudaw.net/english/business/09092024)
- **Jan 28, 2026: Iraq's federal Ministry of Agriculture notified KRI of 32 banned agricultural products** (tomatoes, potatoes, cucumbers, eggplant and others). Erbil's agriculture DG: KRI ships **250–300 truckloads a day** to central and southern Iraq from March to November. — [Kurdistan24 (Sorani), 28 Jan 2026](https://www.kurdistan24.net/ckb/story/890471)
- Feb 2026: Baghdad held up watermelons, potatoes, tomatoes and cucumbers on the Makhmour road, and farmers and traders protested. Minister Begard Talabani said it breaks the agricultural-calendar agreement. KRI exported about **1.3 million t of fruit and vegetables to Iraq in 2024** (the stated value of "8 billion IQD / $5.5M" looks far too low and is probably a reporting error). — [Shafaq, 17 Feb 2026](https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/Baghdad-restricts-agricultural-goods-from-Iraqi-Kurdistan)
- Iraq's import ban on 15 crops: Baghdad says many of them entered via KRI labelled as local produce. — [FreshPlaza](https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9809105/iraq-blocks-imports-of-15-fruit-and-vegetables) (snippet; date not confirmed)
- Responsibility: KRG MoAWR (import bans), KRG border/customs; Iraqi federal Ministry of Agriculture (agricultural calendar, checkpoints).

#### RANK 6 (medium): Crop and field fires
- Erbil, Jan–Jun 2024: **2,513 dunams of wheat and barley burned**, and officials warned farmers. — [Rudaw, 23 Jun 2024](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/230620242)
- Sulaimani, May 2025: about 1,000 dunams of forest and shrubland burned, plus 40 dunams of wheat in Zarayan and 200 dunams at Sitak. — [Rudaw, 28 May 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/28052025) (snippet)
- Turkish shelling started large fires in Duhok's mountains in 2026. — [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/Turkish-artillery-causes-large-wildfire-in-Iraq-s-Duhok) (snippet)
- 2019 wave (older, mostly outside KRG administration): ISIS-claimed arson in disputed areas, including 600+ dunams in Khanaqin/Qaratapa in one week ([Rudaw, 24 May 2019](https://rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/24052019)). Nineveh lost 65,000 dunams by 10 June and Kirkuk 8,500. Iraq's PM said 40,000 dunams nationwide, while ≥145,000 dunams were counted in 10 areas alone. — [The New Humanitarian, Jul 2019](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2019/07/08/crop-fires-sinjar-newly-returned-iraqis) (snippet)
- Scale check: the 2024–25 KRI fires cover thousands of dunams, compared with about 2 million dunams lost to drought in 2025. Fires are well known but about 1,000 times smaller.

#### RANK 7 (medium-low, rising): Pesticide misuse and food safety
- Feb 2026 investigation (200 farmers in Duhok, Nineveh and Basra): **91.5% use imported pesticides, 28.5% admit using restricted or banned products, and 43% cannot identify the product or whether it is registered**. 14% reported family illness. The Ministry of Health said it had recorded no cases. — [KirkukNow, 11 Feb 2026](https://kirkuknow.com/en/news/71519)
- The only food-safety research on Kurdistan noted is a Koya University researcher's book on pesticide residues (older, date not confirmed). — [Koya University](https://koyauniversity.org/node/109)

#### Candidates checked and DROPPED or DOWNGRADED (too small or not confirmed for 2023–2026)
- **Farmland lost to housing:** only individual cases found, e.g. about 1,000 dunams in Ankawa given to a developer ([Assyrian Policy Institute](https://www.assyrianpolicy.org/post/new-case-of-assyrian-owned-lands-in-ankawa-erbil-appropriated-by-the-kurdistan-regional-government), date not confirmed), minority land-distribution complaints in Duhok ([Peregraf](https://peregraf.com/en/report/10849)), and a "Do not turn our land into housing units" protest ([KirkukNow](https://kirkuknow.com/en/news/69319), not opened). There is no region-wide dunam figure, so it is not ranked.
- **Pests (Sunn pest, locusts):** only older evidence: Sunn pest campaigns in Erbil, Duhok and Sulaimani ([WFP document, older](https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000115993/download)) and locusts in Bradost in 2021 ([Rudaw, 2021](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/300520212-amp)). Nothing found for 2023–2026.
- **Youth leaving farming:** the 2025 Rudaw piece on Pishdar/Qaladze youth migration blames unemployment, corruption and politics, not farming, and mentions agriculture only in passing. — [Rudaw, 7 Jul 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/070720252). Not confirmed as a farming problem.
- **Electricity for pumps:** KRI grid supply is under 10–12 h a day and diesel is costly ([AUIS IRIS](https://auis.edu.krd/iris/node/609)), but no farm-specific figures were found.
- **Land ownership disputes:** not found in the 2023–2026 farming coverage.
- **Climate displacement (Iraq-wide, labelled):** IOM counted 31,001 families (186,006 people) displaced by climate factors across 12 provinces (Sept 2025), mostly in the south. — [Shafaq report](https://www.shafaq.com/en/Report/Iraq-s-Displacement-From-war-driven-migration-to-climate-induced-mobility-pressures) (snippet). Not a KRI-specific story.

#### Background size of the sector
- 68,704 winter-crop farmers were recorded in KRSO's 2016–17 survey ([KRSO, 2017](https://krso.gov.krd/content/upload/1/root/winter-crops-survey-20171.pdf), snippet). Government figures cited 153,000 farmers and agriculture at about 10% of GDP (2016, [Rudaw](https://rudaw.net/english/business/07042016), snippet). ">90% of Kurdistan's food is imported" (2015, older; [Rudaw](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/271120151-amp)).

### Inferences
- Ranks 1–4 are one water story: bad rain → reservoirs fall (made worse by Iranian dams) → farmers pump groundwater → wheat fails anyway. Ranks 1 and 2 are also linked: in good years (2024, 2026) the problem flips from "no crop" to "no buyer", because Baghdad's quota is fixed and does not follow actual production.
- By money: wheat is the biggest single item. Even the reduced 2025 purchase was worth about $229M, and the 2025 drought loss of about 800,000 t at 850,000 IQD/t is about 680 billion IQD (about $520M; my calculation from the cited tonnage and price). Fires (thousands of dunams) and pesticides are about 100–1,000 times smaller in measured size.
- The Shafaq quote "quotas since 2017 have not reflected actual production levels" points to a gap: there is no independent, trusted per-province production estimate before harvest.

### Gaps
- No KRSO 2024, 2025 or 2026 crop-production tables could be opened (403). The 2023–24 to 2025–26 wheat area and production by province remains news-based.
- Final 2025 payment status (how much of the 83B IQD was paid) and the final 2026 purchase and payments: not found.
- No KRI-wide figure for farmland converted to housing; no 2023–2026 pest-outbreak data; no farm-specific electricity data.
- Darbandikhan's exact 2025 low level was not found (only Dukan's).

## Q2. Which problems could an AI system realistically help with?

### Takeaway
AI with satellite data fits best where the problem is about *knowing the amount early*: drought/yield forecasting, per-province production estimates (useful in the quota fight), reservoir surface monitoring, irrigated-field and pivot detection (groundwater pressure), and fire detection. It fits poorly for prices, smuggling, payments and pesticides, which are policy or enforcement problems where satellites see nothing useful.

### Cited Findings
- Satellite drought indices work in this area: a Sulaimani Landsat study (NDVI/NDWI/SPI, 1998–2017) found severe droughts in 1999, 2000, 2008, 2009 and 2012, a 33.3% drop in vegetation cover in 2000, and Darbandikhan lake losing 40.5% of its surface area in 2009. — [Univ. of Southampton eprint](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/434617) (snippet)
- AFP itself used satellite imagery to report Dukan's 56% surface shrinkage, which shows reservoir-area monitoring from space already makes headlines. — [AFP via Arab News, Jun 2025](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2605253/middle-east)
- An official complaint says quotas don't match real production ([Shafaq, Jun 2026](https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/85-of-Duhok-wheat-crop-may-remain-unsold)), and the KRG wants Baghdad to buy the full harvest ([Rudaw, Oct 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/14102025)).
- A Halabja tomato article ends: "better exports and coordinated planning could help farmers sell". — [Rudaw search summary, Jul 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/25072025) (snippet)

### Inferences (AI-fit rating per problem)
| Problem | AI/satellite fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Drought / rain-fed wheat failure (R1) | **High** | NDVI, soil moisture and rain anomalies per zone; an early-season risk forecast is technically standard. |
| Wheat quota fight (R2) | **High (as evidence)** | A pre-harvest per-province production estimate from satellite would give KRG MoAWR independent numbers to negotiate with. It cannot fix payments. |
| Reservoirs / Iran dams (R3) | **Medium-high** | Water-surface area from Sentinel-1/2 is reliable; volume needs depth curves; dam directors already know the levels, so the value is public transparency. |
| Groundwater / illegal wells (R4) | **Medium** | Can detect fields irrigated in summer and pivot circles; cannot see wells or measure aquifers; politically sensitive. |
| Prices / imports / Baghdad bans (R5) | **Low-medium** | Planting-area estimates could help coordinate supply, but prices and smuggling are policy and enforcement issues, and price data is scarce. |
| Fires (R6) | **High feasibility, small impact** | NASA FIRMS plus burned-area mapping are off-the-shelf. |
| Pesticides (R7) | **Low** | Not visible from satellites; needs lab testing. |

### Gaps
- No published KRI validation of satellite yield models against KRSO or silo intake figures was found.

## Q3. What does the KRG say it wants? (food self-sufficiency, smart agriculture, SmartSuli, targets)

### Takeaway
The KRG's stated goals are food security and self-sufficiency, cutting imports, and diversifying away from oil through agriculture. "Smart agriculture" appears in official messaging (no-till, aquaponics, "WaterBox", digital registries), but no quantified digital-agriculture or AI target was found. SmartSuli is a data-driven smart-city platform run by Deputy PM Qubad Talabani.

### Cited Findings
- 9th cabinet: agriculture and industry are "key priorities". The ministry promotes smart agriculture, aquaponics, no-till (in some areas from "30 dunams to 300 dunams") and "WaterBox" as an alternative to drip irrigation. Achievements claimed: 30,000 greenhouses, 319 cold stores, and exports to Iraq, Turkey, Jordan and the UAE. Agriculture's investment share went from 1.8% to 10%. Spokesperson: Qaraman Qadir. — [Kurdistan24, 16 Aug 2026](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/932923/krg-accelerates-agricultural-modernization-as-exports-expand)
- Another outlet gives the investment share as 1.8% → **12%** "toward self-sufficiency". — [Channel8](https://channel8.com/english/news/61256) (snippet). This conflicts with the 10% figure above.
- Seeds: 10,000 t of subsidized wheat seed handed out Oct 1–15, 2025 under the "food security and self-sufficiency" strategy ([Kurdistan24](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/opinion/839743/kurdistan-government-launches-subsidized-wheat-seed-distribution-plan-to-support-local-farmers), snippet); 11,000 t for 2026 ([Rudaw, May 2026](https://www.rudaw.net/english/business/05052026)).
- Livestock projects went from 65 to 106 and herds from 21,000 to 128,000 head ([Kurdistan24](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/883283/krg-increases-livestock-projects-from-65-to-106-raising-herds-to-128000-heads), snippet); dairy output doubled to cut imports ([Kurdistan24](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/858932/kurdistan-doubles-dairy-output-to-cut-imports-and-strengthen-food-security), snippet).
- Digital administration: a **digital registry of agricultural workers**, plus digital IDs for agricultural companies (UEN) and projects (UPN), under Council of Ministers Resolution 172. Run by MoAWR with the Labour, Trade and Coordination departments. — [Kurdistan24, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/925733)
- Water storage: 118 ponds built (22.6 million m³, 28.8 billion IQD). Proposed dams: Taq Taq (1 billion m³), Mandawa, Bakrman, Dalga. — [Peregraf, Aug 2025](https://peregraf.com/en/investigation/9492)
- **SmartSuli:** a platform launched by DPM Qubad Talabani to solve Sulaimani's problems through "smart solutions, gathering data, utilizing advanced technology", inviting citizens and experts to help ([PUKmedia](https://www.pukmedia.com/EN/Details/76251)). It sits alongside a "Data Revolution Initiative" announced at a MEED conference ([PUKmedia](https://pukmedia.com/EN/Details/76126)). The first SmartSuli hackathon tackled health-sector problems ([PUKmedia](https://pukmedia.com/EN/Details/76253)). Program page: [foundation.krd](https://foundation.krd/node/6).
- Iraq-wide (labelled): the federal agriculture ministry is developing "smart agriculture fields" and smart irrigation to cut water use by up to 40%, tied to a $1.3B Green Climate Fund programme (18 projects, May 2025). — [Rudaw, 18 Aug 2025](https://www.rudaw.net/english/middleeast/iraq/180820251)

### Inferences
- An AI tool that supports **self-sufficiency, data transparency, and evidence for the Baghdad negotiations** matches both the KRG's stated goals and SmartSuli's "data to find solutions" pitch.

### Gaps
- No published KRG MoAWR digital or smart-agriculture strategy document with targets was found. No official KRG self-sufficiency percentage target was found.

## Q4. Existing digital/AI agriculture work in KRI and Iraq: who, what, did it work?

### Takeaway
The most serious remote-sensing work in KRI is **FAO's WaPOR** (water productivity from satellites), which includes KRI rain-fed and irrigated areas and was demonstrated in the Erbil Basin in May 2025. Farmer-facing apps exist only as pilots or private schemes: the WFP and Iraqi MoA E-Farming app (piloted in Kerbala), a USAID-backed satellite app in Nineveh, and Hydrosat for Beirut Erbil's contract potato farmers. University work is at the dataset stage (watermelon and fig disease images). No public results show region-wide adoption by KRI farmers, and no Sorani farmer-facing AI tool was found.

### Cited Findings
- **FAO WaPOR phase II** with the Iraqi Ministry of Water Resources covers West Al-Gharraf **and the Kurdistan Region (rain-fed and irrigated)**. Ministry staff used WaPOR data in reports "approved by governmental decision-makers". — [UN Iraq / FAO press release](https://iraq.un.org/en/node/219142) (snippet; node number not confirmed)
- FAO Director-General Qu Dongyu visited KRI on **26 May 2025**. He met the KRI President, the Deputy PM and the agriculture minister on "climate-resilient agriculture, sustainable water management, **digital innovation**", and saw a **WaPOR field demonstration in the Erbil Basin with farmers from Jazhnakan village**. No signed agreements were reported. — [FAO](https://www.fao.org/director-general/news/details/in-iraq--director-general-co-launches--39-million-climate-resilience-project/en)
- FAO completed a two-phase programme building Iraq's **Land Degradation Monitoring Platform** (field data, satellite imagery, digital maps). — [UN Iraq](https://iraq.un.org/en/node/277583) (snippet)
- FAO's $39M SRVALI (GCF) climate project targets the south (Karbala, Najaf, Muthanna), not KRI. — [FAO](https://www.fao.org/director-general/news/details/in-iraq--director-general-co-launches--39-million-climate-resilience-project/en)
- **WFP + Iraqi MoA "E-Farming" app:** meant to centralize farmer data and improve information and market access for **650,000+ farmers across Iraq**. It was approved and deployed for testing, with a pilot in **Kerbala**. — WFP country reporting ([ReliefWeb, WFP Iraq brief](https://reliefweb.int/report/iraq/wfp-iraq-country-brief-may-2024), snippet). In 2022 WFP tendered for a smallholder digital platform in English, Arabic and **Kurdish** (extension, weather, market linkage). — [UNGM notice](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/170092) (snippet)
- **USAID-supported satellite farm-monitoring app, Nineveh** (wheat and corn): disease alerts, treatment tips, weather and a farmer forum; claims production costs fall by "up to 20%" (an unverified claim; no app name, partner or user count published). — [Iraq Business News, 3 May 2024](https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/?p=218832)
- USAID + Top Mountain **Agribusiness Incubator and Accelerator** for KRI agri start-ups. — [Iraq Business News, Agritech tag](https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/tag/agritech/) (snippet; date not confirmed)
- **Private sector:** Beirut Erbil Potato Products adopted **Hydrosat** (satellite soil moisture, water stress, disease signals, irrigation and fertilizer advice) for its contracted farmers, around Dec 2025. — [PotatoPro](https://www.potatopro.com/node/169866)
- **GIZ + University of Duhok:** €190,773 vocational training in vegetables and orchards for 820 Domiz refugees and 170 local farmers (not digital). — [University of Duhok](https://uod.ac/about/presidency/directorate-of-international-relations/project/giz-capacity-building/?lang=en). A Netherlands/CABI potato value-chain project also runs with UoD. — [RVO](https://projects.rvo.nl/projects/nl-kvk-27378529-sdgp2329iq)
- **University datasets:** Kurdistan Watermelon Disease dataset (Halabja, Penjwin, Sharazur): 1,152 images in 5 classes, augmented to 10,368. — [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/4dyy62bvfp). Kurdish Fig Leaf Dataset from Sartak Valley near Sulaimani. — [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/875dysnh7g)
- An AI conference was held in Erbil in June 2024, described as Iraq's first. — [The New Region](https://thenewregion.com/posts/666) (snippet)

### Inferences
- **Gap the hackathon can fill:** WaPOR is an expert tool for ministries (English, technical). Hydrosat serves one company's contract farmers. E-Farming is a federal Iraq pilot outside KRI. None of them is a free, public, Sorani tool that ranks KRI zones by drought risk or gives per-province production estimates. A hackathon team should say it **builds on** open satellite data like WaPOR, and should not claim to be the first satellite tool in KRI.
- University disease datasets exist for Sharazur-area crops, but they need phone photos, not satellites, so they don't fit a satellite-only scope.

### Gaps
- No evaluations or results found for WaPOR in KRI, E-Farming's rollout, the Nineveh app's user numbers, or the USAID incubator. The effect of the 2025 US foreign-aid cuts on USAID agriculture work in Iraq was not checked.
- Nothing found from JICA, iMMAP or UNDP on agricultural AI or digital work in KRI. No University of Sulaimani or Salahaddin operational AI farming project was found (only datasets and academic drought studies).
