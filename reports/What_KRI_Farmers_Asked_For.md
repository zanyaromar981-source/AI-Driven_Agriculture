## Ministry and channel facts
- Ministry of Agriculture: "smart agriculture" line (Dec 2025); a digital registry of the farm labour force announced 14 Jul 2026 (kurdistan24.net/en/story/925733); 2026 farmer registration needs a bank number; electronic monitoring exists only for 5,400 drinking-water wells (en.964media.com/30932).
- Extension: ~215 staff, the system rated "weak" (UHD 2024). Sulaymaniyah extension workers' attitude to AI: neutral-to-negative, 99 surveyed; **smartphone ranked the #1 tool** (KUJAS 2024).
- Channels farmers use: Facebook advisers (Azad Muhamad ~500k followers, asked about insects, fruit trees, yields, spacing). No farmer hotline found. No Kurdish version of FAO's Al-Rafidain or WFP's e-Farming found.
- SmartSuli 2026 is framed only as "bring a real problem from your city"; nothing specific on agriculture was found.

## What a 48-hour AI prototype can credibly answer
1. **Evidence for the quota fight (#2):** Sentinel-2 wheat area and condition per district, the "scientific basis" the Ministry itself says is missing. Strongest fit: the Ministry already asked for it.
2. **Glut early warning (#4, #5):** planted-area detection for tomato/cucumber/potato + prices, pushed to farmers' councils like Penjwen.
3. **Well and drought stress maps (#6, #9):** for Erbil and Garmiyan.
4. **Kurdish photo diagnosis and rust alerts (#10):** matches what farmers ask advisers; extension staff rank the smartphone first.
5. Not credible in 48 h: payments, prices, quotas themselves, cold stores, subsidies (#1, #3, #7, #8): money and politics.
