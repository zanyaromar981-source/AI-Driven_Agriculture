# Kurdistan Region: problems in society (130 problems, 10 areas)

Researched 2026-09-11 by 10 research agents (~310 web searches: news, UN/NGO reports, KRG releases, academic studies). One source per problem is shown here. The full tables with every source are in `evidence/problems_by_area/`.
Fit = how well a city/government AI system could help (High / Med / Low).

## Best 10 for SmartSuli (city systems, like last year's bus winner)

| # | Problem | Slemani evidence | Who owns the fix | What the system does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Water comes only every 5 days; 15 neighbourhoods have no pipes** | supply cut to every 5 days; 120 of 160 springs/karez dry; 15 neighbourhoods get a tanker once a week; the new pipeline ($424M) is years away | Slemani Water Directorate | tells each street when water comes, predicts when home tanks run dry, routes tankers, sorts leak/waste reports |
| 2 | **Flash floods** | Dec 2025: 2 dead, 1,607 homes damaged in Slemani province; 850 students out of school | Municipality, Civil Defence, crisis centre | rain forecast + street slopes + citizen photos of blocked drains → street-level alerts. The rainy season starts right after the hackathon |
| 3 | **Internet shut down on exam days to stop leaks** | 16 Kurdistan-only shutdowns in 2025, everyone offline for hours | Ministry of Education + Communications | gives each exam hall a slightly different paper; when a leaked photo appears on Telegram, AI finds which hall it came from. Catch the leaker instead of cutting everyone's internet |
| 4 | **ATMs empty on salary day** | ~800k people on salary cards, ~572 ATMs; the KRG publicly blamed the bank for not refilling Slemani ATMs (Sep 2025) | Finance ministry (MyAccount) + banks | live map of which ATMs have cash + forecast of when each runs out |
| 5 | **No smart ambulance dispatch in Slemani** | Erbil launched GPS nearest-ambulance dispatch in Nov 2025; no Slemani version found (verify) | Slemani Health Directorate (122) | caller's Kurdish description → location → nearest ambulance → fastest route |
| 6 | **Stray dogs** | ~1,000 bites a year in KRI; Slemani's $2-per-dog bounty campaign turned abusive (Oct 2025) | Municipality + vets | citizen photos → AI counts dogs → hotspot map for humane catch-and-vaccinate teams |
| 7 | **Spoiled and expired food** | 817 kg of spoiled walnuts traced from a Slemani sweets factory; imports mostly untested | Slemani Health Directorate (food control) | citizens photograph products; AI reads expiry dates and ranks which shops inspectors visit first |
| 8 | **Children working and begging in the streets** | 578 registered in Slemani (2025); another count says 2,600+ child workers in the city | Social Affairs + Kurdistan Save the Children | sighting reports (no photos of faces) → AI merges duplicates → cases routed to social workers |
| 9 | **Winter fuel doesn't reach people who need it** | kerosene: 111M litres given vs. 257M needed; 17,000+ disabled people in Slemani without winter fuel; gas cylinder price doubled | Natural Resources + neighbourhood mukhtars | fair need-based allocation + flags duplicate/fraud claims |
| 10 | **Air pollution is back** | generators restarted during the 2026 war; Slemani fine dust at ~6x the WHO limit in a live reading | Environment Board | neighbourhood air/dust forecast → alerts for asthma patients and schools |

**Still valid from the first round:** Dosya (patient-held health records; SmartSuli's own 2024 challenge) and Rêber (government paperwork guide with a document check).

**Verify before pitching any of these:**
- #1: early-2026 rains refilled the dams, so check how bad the rotation is right now.
- #5: confirm that Slemani really has no GPS dispatch.
- The "cash salaries ended 31 Aug" date: sources conflict on 2025 vs. 2026.
- Rows with 2020–2021 evidence (marked "old") need a fresh check.
