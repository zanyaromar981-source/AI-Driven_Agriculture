# Evidence: city/public services + other domains (two agent reports, condensed)

## Government services: MEDIUM evidence, universal pain
- Federal Ur portal: only 352 of 708 listed services are electronic; 2.3M users (Jan 2024). https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/2024/01/21/iraq-expands-e-government-services-portal/
- The KRG promised "any service, anywhere, any device" by 2025, and missed it. https://gov.krd/dxs/
- KRDPass digital ID (beta 2025): 100k users in 5 days; only a few services (Hajj, passport renewal, salary check, company records). https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/kurdistan-reaches-digital-id-milestone-krd-pass-expanding-reach
- **services.gov.krd has static pages listing the requirements per service → scrapable knowledge base.** e.g. https://services.gov.krd/en/service/moi-58-en
- e-Psûle: online utility bills after "decades" of queues. https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/920101
- Muaqib (paid paperwork agent) pain: anecdotal only → gather our own stories.

## Water: STRONG in 2025, recovered in 2026
- Dukan at ~24% of capacity in June 2025; rainfall 220 mm vs. a normal 600 mm; Iran dams the Little Zab. https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/06/water-levels-plummet-drought-hit-iraqi-reservoir
- Dec 2025 storms refilled the dams; 2026 inflow was 7x the year before → frame as "the next drought".
- No dam telemetry is public; Sentinel-2 imagery is free.

## Electricity: strong pain, but the cause is geopolitical
- Runaki: 85%+ of households had 24h power by Apr 2026; 1.345M smart meters; overcharges refunded. Khor Mor strikes (Nov 2025, Feb 2026) → blackouts. Software can't fix it.

## Air, traffic, waste
- Air: weak in Slemani (generators are being removed). Road deaths: falling 60% over 5 yrs thanks to speed cameras. Tanjaro landfill: unlined, 1,200–1,300 t/day, no open data.

## Legal access: need MEDIUM, data STRONG
- **The Parliament law database is searchable in Kurdish + Arabic, plain text.** https://www.parliament.krd/english/parliament-business/legislation/
- UNDP/EU legal help desks in Erbil/Slemani/Duhok served ~7,300 people. No plain-Sorani explainer of the law exists.

## Other domains
- **Digital blackmail: STRONG.** 13,000+ complaints of digital harassment/blackmail in 2024 (sources differ on Iraq-wide vs. KRI Community Police); WOLA: 226 cases in KRI in 2024 (107 in Duhok); KRI Law 8/2011 doesn't cover digital crimes; 21% of Iraqi women report online violence. https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/16-Day-Action-for-Women-Kurdistan-pushes-back-against-online-threats
- **Deaf: STRONG pain, weak 48h fit.** ~10,000 deaf people, FEWER THAN 5 sign interpreters in KRI (2024); deaf education stops at grade 6; KuSL differs by city; AUIS Slemani teaches KuSL with a deaf instructor (2024). https://www.auis.edu.krd/News_all/deaf-studies-and-kurdish-sign-language-offered-first-time-auis
- **Used cars: MEDIUM-STRONG.** "Iraq a dumping ground for damaged cars" (Rudaw, 24 Dec 2025); 170,000+ vehicles imported into KRI (Jan 2025); the KRG 2026 import policy bans burnt/submerged cars; the only VIN report found is English-only (vininfohub, $4.99). https://www.rudaw.net/english/middleeast/iraq/24122025
- Language barrier Arabic↔Kurdish: only old evidence (2013–2016). Tourism: booming, not a pain. Addresses: weak evidence. Landmines: strong pain, AI can't validate in 48h. Drugs: strong pain, risky. Migration: an app can't change the economics.
