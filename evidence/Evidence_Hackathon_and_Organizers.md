# Evidence: hackathon + ecosystem intel (agent report, condensed)

## Who's behind it (matters a lot)
- **SmartSuli** is a smart-city program started by Deputy PM **Qubad Talabani** and hosted by The Foundation, a "community-driven platform" for citizens to co-create smart-city solutions for Sulaymaniyah. Sources: https://pukmedia.com/EN/Details/76253 (Jan 2024), https://esta.krd/en/286686/, https://foundation.krd/smartsuli/smart-suli
- **SmartSuli Hackathon'24** (7 Jan 2024; teams of 2–5; 10 days; shown at the IIEF forum on 25 Jan 2024) had two challenges:
  - **HEALTH:** there's no shared patient-data system, so patients repeat tests at different clinics
  - **OPEN:** any real city problem
  - Source: https://www.smartsuli.krd/en/hackathon. Winners and whether anything got used: UNKNOWN.
- **The Foundation** (foundation.krd): "Kurdistan's Foundation for private sector, rural area and economic development". President Safeen Ghafour is a Senior Advisor to the DPM. It runs The Foundation Hub (1,500 m², free desks, startup support), the IIEF forum (2024, 2025) and a DIFC Innovation Hub Dubai partnership (mentors, investors, Dubai AI Festival).
- **Partners** are all IRADA Network members:
  - Tafa3ul Hub (Basra): youth/startup/SME programs
  - QAF Lab (Mosul): incubator, small grants, leans agri (Sadara Farm, Golden Mushroom)
  - Makers of Baghdad: FabLab + incubator since 2017, USAID-backed, 4,300+ trained
- No published judging criteria were found. Prizes at comparable Iraqi hackathons are small ($350–$6,000), so incubation is the real prize.

## Opportunity spotted
MetKurd / KI etc. are BUILDING BLOCKS we can call (Sorani STT/TTS/OCR), not competitors, as long as our product is a workflow, not "a Kurdish chatbot".

## Twist on the 2024 health challenge
The 2024 challenge was "no shared patient data → repeated tests". The classic answer (a hospital-side shared record) needs every clinic to adopt a system, which is why these projects die. A patient-held record flips it: the family's phone IS the record, and AI reads the papers they already get (lab reports, prescriptions, scan reports). No clinic integration is needed on day 1.
