# Evidence: economy / commerce / money (agent report, condensed)

## Salary delays: STRONG (the root cause; political, not app-fixable)
- July 2015 to June 2025: salaries were late or partial in 62 of 120 months (18 unpaid, 34 partial). https://theamargi.com/posts/a-decade-of-unpaid-wages-iraqi-kurdistans-salary-crisis
- Arrears: about IQD 30.8 trillion (~$21bn). https://thenationalcontext.com/krg-salary-arrears-21-billion-decade/
- Payroll: about 1.19M recipients. https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/922224
- 2026 is still late: June salaries were funded on 13 July; July salaries on 17 Aug "after weeks of delay". https://www.iraqinews.com/iraq/baghdad-transfers-841-billion-dinars-to-krg-for-public-worker-wages/ and https://dckurd.org/2026/08/27/kurdistan-digest-august-2026/
- Effects: markets crowded but nobody buys, strikes in Slemani, flea-market sales. https://peregraf.com/en/report/9165 , https://en.964media.com/24106/

## Shop debt notebooks: MEDIUM (one strong article, needs our own interviews)
- Rudaw, Duhok, 12 Sep 2023: one shopkeeper keeps 3 debt notebooks; "around 70% or more" of purchases are on credit; debts run IQD 10k to 1M; bakers' books are overflowing too. https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/12092023
- Erbil markets are hit the same way. https://www.rudaw.net/english/kurdistan/051020231
- Competitors: only generic Arabic debt apps ("دفتر ديوني" on the Iraqi App Store, Karni Mob, Seelfa). NO Sorani-first ledger, NO payday reminders, NO pay-by-FIB/FastPay/Qi link.

## Digital payments: STRONG (the shift is happening now)
- **Cash salaries ended 31 Aug 2026.** MyAccount: 900,600 registered by Sep 2025; 760k+ paid through it by May 2026; ~150k cards not yet collected and 25k employees unregistered at the cutoff. https://channel8.com/english/news/63577 , https://gov.krd/mofe-en/activities/news-and-press-releases/2026/may/over-760-000-public-sector-beneficiaries-are-paid-through-myaccount/
- CBI: all government payments electronic by July 2026; card machines mandatory in ~17,000 pharmacies; retail card payments up +41% year on year (Q3 2025). https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/2025/12/20/retail-e-payments-via-local-cards-rise-in-q3-2025/
- Qi Card 10–11M users; ZainCash 1M+ users; FIB is a digital bank with account opening in under 5 minutes; under 20% of adults had a bank account in 2021.

## Scams: MEDIUM (many cases, few numbers)
- 13,000+ online harassment/blackmail complaints in 2024 (Iraq). https://www.stimson.org/2025/cybercrime-is-iraqs-next-big-challenge/
- "Operation Code" (Oct 2025 to Feb 2026, with Interpol): 201 arrests for phishing/fraud. https://en.hathalyoum.net/articles/268904
- Fake ZainCash sites run via paid Instagram ads (one victim lost ~$1,400); Qi Card fake-call scams; forex/USDT scams in Erbil. https://en.964media.com/16760/
- No Kurdish "is this a scam?" checker found. About 1M salary earners are on bank cards now, many for the first time.

## Social commerce: MEDIUM (big, but AI DM bots ALREADY EXIST)
- About 80% of Iraqi e-commerce runs via social media; over 90% is cash on delivery (blog estimates). https://voxire.com/blog/iraqi-ecommerce-market-entry-2026/
- **Competitors already doing Sorani/Iraqi-Arabic DM bots: RABT Labs (IG/Messenger/WhatsApp), Baaga (Kurdistan/Iraq), POTOO.** https://rabtlabs.com/ , https://baaga.site/en
- The open gaps are fake/refused COD orders and courier cash reconciliation, which need data we don't have in 48h.

## Not recommended
Jobs (the market is crowded; the problem is too few jobs, not matching), agriculture (a policy/water problem; hard-to-reach users).
