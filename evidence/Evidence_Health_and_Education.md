# Evidence: health + education (agent report, condensed)

## Health
- **Paper records: MEDIUM.** Iraq has no national digital record; KRI authors call it paper-based and inefficient (Oxford Open Digital Health, 2025). https://academic.oup.com/oodh/article/doi/10.1093/oodh/oqaf023/8269851
  - The KRG MoH + Kurdistan Innovation Institute launched a region-wide digital HOSPITAL system (~May 2026) for public hospitals. https://shafaq.com/en/Kurdistan/Kurdistan-unveils-first-Region-wide-digital-hospital-system
  - Private evening clinics and labs are likely NOT connected (inference) → a patient-held record avoids the integration problem.
- **Lab results in English: WEAK evidence, real gap.** No Sorani/Badini lab explainer found. Arabic ones exist (Biolab Jordan "AI Result Interpreter"). Comprehension pain unverified → interview ~10 patients + lab staff.
- **Clinic booking: CROWDED.** 17 booking startups in Iraq (Tracxn, Jan 2026): Slaw Doctor, The Doctor KRD, Tabibiraq, Hijozaty, Doctoury, Hamu… → idea #3 is dead.
- **Antibiotics & counterfeits: STRONG.**
  - A 2026 simulated-customer study of 696 pharmacies in 5 Iraqi cities: antibiotics sold without a prescription in >80% of visits, ~40% of them WHO "Watch" class, almost no counselling. https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14787210.2026.2663104
  - Iraqi Pharmacists Syndicate: 35% of drugs are counterfeit or smuggled. https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/828757
  - Smuggled medicines seized at Said Sadiq (Slemani), ~Mar 2024.
  - Existing: the Gudea national track-and-trace QR system (authenticity). Gap: patient education in Kurdish; a photo can't detect fakes.
- **Mental health: STRONG pain, UNSAFE for a 48h prototype.** ~0.34 psychiatrists per 100k in Iraq. Self-immolation is 38.3% of burn admissions in Sulaymaniyah, the highest in Iraq. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37139597/
- Medical travel and hospital overcrowding: weak/old data.

## Education
- **Grade 12: STRONG.** 117,142 candidates in 2026 (https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/919824/), pass rate under 50% (2025 study). Zankoline places 97% of applicants, so the score decides the DEPARTMENT. https://en.964media.com/41233/
- **Teacher strikes (Slemani): STRONG.** Early 2025: "600,000 pupils of 3,000 schools deprived of education for two months" (https://www.iraqicivilsociety.org/archives/14172); a new boycott announced Sep 2025.
- **Tutoring cost:** no data found.
- **COMPETITORS: Rahenan** (https://rahenan.app/en/): an AI tutor on the KRI curriculum; photo → step-by-step in Sorani/Badini/English; uses past ministerial questions; 1,000+ downloads. Also Kurdistan Academy (grade-12 e-learning), Kurdistan AI, the "مناهج كردستان" app (all textbooks). → exam coach #6 has LOW novelty.
- MoE digital: E-Assessment (grades, parent messages), the "My School" platform. None are tutoring tools.
