Score guide: AUC = chance the model gives a bad zone-season a higher risk than a good one (0.50 = coin flip, 1.00 = perfect).
catch@15%FA = share of bad zone-seasons caught when false alarms are held at 15%.  Brier skill > 0 = better than always saying 25%.
"vs current" = in how many of 1000 reshuffles of the seasons this beats the current model (≥ 0.90 means probably real).
